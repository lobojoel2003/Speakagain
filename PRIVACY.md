# Privacy

Speakagain is made for people recovering from a stroke or brain injury, and for their families and
therapists. It is built so that nothing about them leaves the device.

- **No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.** Speakagain does not collect or send any data.
- **Speech stays on the device.** When speech checking is on, the microphone records only after the
  person taps it, and only until they stop talking. The recording is checked on the device by the
  speech model, kept in memory until the next word (so *Hear me* can play it back; on iPhone the
  audio player briefly uses a temporary file), and never saved with progress or sent anywhere.
- **Practice history stays on the device**: settings, which words were practised and when, and
  packs added from files. Android's own backup includes them (not the downloaded models); turn
  device backup off if that isn't wanted. *Settings › Clear practice history* deletes the history.
- **The internet is only used when the person chooses** to download speech checking or a natural
  voice. Those files come from this project's GitHub releases over HTTPS, and GitHub sees an
  ordinary download request. In a web browser, the app itself is loaded from where it is hosted.
- **The phone's voice** uses the device's own text-to-speech engine; on Android that engine is
  chosen by the person in the system settings.

Questions: open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/lobojoel2003/Speakagain/issues), without
personal details.
