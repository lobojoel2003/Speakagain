# Code of conduct

Speakagain is made for and with people who have aphasia: people who may speak, read or write with
difficulty, slowly, or with help. Everyone taking part in this project (issues, pull requests,
discussions) is expected to:

- be kind and patient, especially with people who communicate differently, slowly or with help;
- talk about people with aphasia with respect: they are the experts on their own communication;
- welcome questions from families, carers and therapists, who may not be programmers;
- give and take feedback about the work, never about the person;
- not harass, demean or exclude anyone, in public or in private.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer privately through the email on their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/lobojoel2003), or use GitHub's
[report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
tools. Reports are handled confidentially. People who break this code may be warned, have content
removed, or be blocked from the project.
